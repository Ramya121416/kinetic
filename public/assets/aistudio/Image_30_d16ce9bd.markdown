## Brand & Style

This design system reimagines high-touch retail and editorial commerce through a tranquil, reductive lens. Replacing aggressive velocity badges and hyper-saturated pigments, the experience centers on tactile quietude, unhurried curation, and understated confidence. It caters to a discerning audience that prioritizes craft, material integrity, and considered living.

The visual direction merges **Warm Editorial Minimalism** with **Tactile Restraint**:
- **Warm Minimalist Surfaces:** Gentle, breathable negative space underpinned by warm alabaster and soft ecru, eliminating sterile pure whites and harsh digital synthetic hues.
- **Editorial Poise:** Authoritative, bookish typography paired with neutral, disciplined layout grids reminiscent of independent quarterly print journals.
- **Subtle Materiality:** Low-contrast container separation, whisper-light hairline borders, and nuanced tonal shifts rather than dramatic shadows or neon alerts.

## Colors

The color architecture moves away from electric saturation in favor of muted luxury tones, drawing inspiration from natural mineral pigments, archival paper, and soft botanical dyes:

- **Primary (`#1E232A` - Slate Charcoal):** The grounding pigment for high-contrast headlines, primary interactive states, and authoritative structural elements.
- **Secondary (`#4A5D4E` - Muted Sage):** A quiet, organic accent used selectively for affirmative states, sustainable provenance markers, and subdued progress markers.
- **Tertiary (`#5A6265` - Warm Taupe Graphite):** An understated neutral tone for secondary iconography, subtle section dividers, and supporting metadata.
- **Neutral (`#FBFBFA` - Soft Ecru Canvas):** The foundational warm alabaster background, bringing an organic, paper-like warmth to the overall canvas.
- **Functional Named Surfaces:**
  - `surface-subtle` (`#F4F4F0`): Delineates embedded cards, lookbook backdrops, and secondary section containers.
  - `surface-border` (`#E4E4DE`): Gentle, barely-there framing lines that maintain form without visual weight.
  - `text-muted` (`#6E757C`): Balanced mid-tone for secondary body copy, captions, and fine-print legal tokens.
  - `slate-graphite` (`#2C343E`): An elevated intermediary tone for interactive states, focused states, and tertiary buttons.

## Typography

The typographic system creates an elevated, editorial tone by contrasting a literary serif headline family with an open, disciplined geometric sans-serif:

- **Editorial Serifs (`Newsreader`):** Applied to headlines, hero moments, and curatorial pull quotes. Rendered with natural proportional spacing and moderate optical weights to evoke high-end monograph publishing.
- **Functional Interface (`Manrope`):** Deployed for all operational body text, metadata, form fields, and labels. Its clean letterforms ensure effortless legibility across dense product descriptions and tabular spec sheets.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** Capitalized metadata labels require deliberate letter-spacing (`0.08em`) with medium font weights (`600`) to guarantee legibility at small sizes over warm ecru surfaces.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model emphasizes composure, spatial breathing room, and structured structural rhythms:

- **Grid Architecture:** 
  - **Mobile (360px - 767px):** 4-column fluid layout with `1.25rem` outer margins. Content blocks maintain tight semantic groupings with breathing margins between sections.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout with `2rem` gutters and margins, facilitating side-by-side editorial panels.
  - **Desktop (1025px+):** 12-column fixed grid with an inner maximum bounds of `1280px` and `3rem` margins to ensure wide margins that frame content symmetrically.
- **Vertical Spacing Cadence:** Built on an 8px base rhythm. Micro adjustments leverage `space-xs` and `space-sm` for tightly bound label-input groups, while full editorial sections use generous `space-xl` intervals to establish a calm, gallery-like viewing tempo.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is maintained through disciplined surface layering and delicate borders rather than heavy drop shadows:

- **Base Layer:** The soft ecru canvas (`#FBFBFA`) grounds the foundational interface.
- **Tonal Inset & Layering:** Elevated components, curated product cells, and utility drawers rest on warm alabaster (`#F4F4F0`) bounded by razor-thin, low-contrast hairline borders (`#E4E4DE`, 1px solid).
- **Floating Modals & Sheets:** Floating panels employ an airy, ambient drop shadow subtly tinted by the charcoal primary tone: `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(30, 35, 42, 0.05)`, backed by a 1px soft border.
- **Backdrop Overlays:** Full-bleed modals leverage a muted translucent overlay (`rgba(30, 35, 42, 0.35)`) accompanied by a subtle backdrop blur (4px) to retain an organic, serene depth.

## Shapes

The geometric architecture uses soft, restrained corners (`roundedness: 1`), providing an architectural silhouette with minimal softening:

- **Standard Elements (0.25rem / 4px):** Applied to interactive buttons, text inputs, selectors, and utility trays for a crisp, tailored finish.
- **Editorial Cards & Imagery (0.5rem / 8px):** Provides gentle framing for collection photography and contextual containers.
- **Pill Exceptions:** Subtle status badges and tag filters may leverage full rounded pill geometries to delineate functional metadata from rectangular imagery containers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid Slate Charcoal (`#1E232A`) fill with Soft Ecru (`#FBFBFA`) typography (`Manrope`, 13px, weight 500). Padding: `0.75rem 1.5rem`. Smooth hover transition to Slate Graphite (`#2C343E`).
- **Secondary Action:** Transparent base framed by a 1px border in `#E4E4DE`. Slate Charcoal text. Hover shifts background to `#F4F4F0`.
- **Tertiary / Curatorial Link:** Unbordered text link in Charcoal with an animated 1px underline offset by 4px, rendered in Muted Sage (`#4A5D4E`).

### Chips & Tags
- **Metadata Tag:** Rendered in Warm Alabaster (`#F4F4F0`) with a 1px `#E4E4DE` border. Typography in Slate Graphite (`#2C343E`), 11px uppercase with `0.08em` letter-spacing.
- **Status Indicator (e.g., In Atelier / Ready):** Soft tint of Muted Sage with subtle border, featuring a 6px solid sage dot alongside concise status text.

### Cards & Curatorial Tiles
- **Product Tiles:** Framed on an ecru base with 4:5 vertical proportions. Surfaces employ `#F4F4F0` image backgrounds. Product details utilize `Newsreader` for titles and `Manrope` for subtle graphite pricing.
- **Story / Editorial Feature:** Subtle 1px bordered containers with generous internal padding (`space-lg`), separating long-form curatorial stories from transaction grids.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- **Form Controls:** Clean alabaster surfaces (`#F4F4F0`) framed by a 1px border (`#E4E4DE`). On focus, smoothly transitions to a clean 1px border in `#1E232A` without harsh glow rings.
- **Selection Radios & Checkboxes:** Refined square and circular controls with custom 1.5px `#6E757C` outlines. Selected states fill with `#1E232A` featuring crisp white glyphs.

### Curatorial Drawer / Try-On Panel
- Replaces high-stress countdown docks with a discreet slide-over tray surfaced in `#FBFBFA`. Item status is managed via quiet segmented toggles (`Retain` / `Return`) rendered in low-contrast warm taupe and slate tones.
